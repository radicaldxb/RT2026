## 1. Current state

### Production (`main` / Netlify)

- Insights, portfolio, chat, services, about, playbook, roadmap, etc.
- **Homepage:** `src/app/home/index.js` — continuous sticky narrative (all beats in one scroll lane).

### V3 homepage prototype (`rt2026-v3-homepage`)

- **Route:** `/landing` — noindex preview of the next homepage approach.
- **Committed:** `landing.js`, `page.js`, `globals.css` scroll/animation utilities.
- **Full spec:** see **[docs/LANDING_V2.md](./docs/LANDING_V2.md)** — architecture, beat timing, styling, formula cards, do-not-regress list.

**Summary of the new approach:**

- **Sticky scroll (beats 1–6):** opening question → dream → *Then.* → reality drift → the gap → formula payoff (*Bold ideas land.*).
- **Normal page flow (after sticky):** formula cards fan/flip (desktop) or fade-up (mobile), then Let's get started, How We Work, Playbook, Proof, Insights, Talk to Us, **What we think** closing quote.
- **Split rationale:** precise scroll timing for the story opening; simpler editing and better mobile behavior for everything after the formula.

```bash
# → http://localhost:3000/landing
```
