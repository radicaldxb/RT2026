# Radical Thinking Brand Guidelines

## 1. Background of Radical Thinking

Radical Thinking is a partner for organisations working on bold ideas. We help clients define what is worth building, design the experience that makes it land, and use technology and AI to amplify both.

Our positioning is grounded in a simple idea: technology and AI are accelerators, not the purpose. The purpose is the outcome your customers actually value.

### Engagement model (high level)

- **The Pulse**: map the gap between intention and customer reality.
- **The Bridge**: deliver a production-ready solution.
- **The Navigator**: keep the capability current through ongoing direction.

## 2. Style of writing

These guidelines apply across the website, marketing copy, and internal structured prompts.

### Voice and tone

- Confident, senior, and practical.
- Editorial and restrained. Prefer clear statements over hype.
- Honest about trade-offs. Avoid inflated claims.
- Assume the reader is smart and busy.

### Message structure (what to say)

- Lead with what matters, then explain how we do it.
- Use outcome language over process language.
- Treat “AI” as a capability. Treat “bold ideas” as the reason it exists.

### Reference the Bold Ideas positioning

Use the “Bold Ideas” framing consistently:

- **Bold Ideas = Creative + Experience × Technology²**
- **BI = C + Ex × T²**

When you mention the model, keep it simple:

- Creative is the human part of the idea.
- Experience is the feeling left behind after every interaction.
- Technology squared is the amplifier that carries and multiplies both.

### Form and clarity (how to write)

- Prefer short paragraphs. Use single sentences for emphasis.
- Use concrete nouns (customer, outcome, experience, capability, system).
- Avoid vague adverbs and filler phrasing.
- Use commas or full stops for emphasis. Avoid em dashes in copy.
- Use consistent capitalization (for example: “Pulse”, “Bridge”, “Navigator”, “Bold Ideas”).

### Examples of on-brand phrasing

- “Creative finds what is worth building.”
- “Experience makes it land.”
- “Technology and AI amplify both.”
- “The real competitor is the thing that can be prompted away.”

## 3. High level design direction of the Radical Thinking brand and website

This section is intentionally high level. For ambient background, fonts, weights, and colour usage, use the website styling guide: [`STYLING_GUIDE.md`](./STYLING_GUIDE.md). Broader design patterns live in [`DESIGN.md`](./DESIGN.md).

### Core visual language

- Warm off-white canvas, calm contrast, and editorial hierarchy.
- Minimalist layouts with clear typographic rhythm.
- Hairline separators and generous whitespace to keep content readable.

### Typography

- Body and UI: **Roboto**.
- Editorial headings and display: **Roboto Slab**.
- Terminal or chat moments: use **system monospace** for the terminal aesthetic (do not introduce extra branded fonts).

### Brand structure and layout beats

- Use formula-aligned sectioning and repeatable narrative flows.
- Prefer “named sections” (Pulse, Bridge, Navigator, Bold Ideas) that map directly to the service story.
- Keep case studies credible. The structure should feel like work, not promotion.

### Motion and interaction

- Use motion to reinforce reading flow, not distract.
- Motion should be subtle, timed to content reveal, and consistent across pages.

### Chat and “terminal” aesthetic

- The chat UI is intentionally terminal-like: black surfaces, monospaced styling, and lightweight UI chrome.
- The chat should feel functional and distinct, while the rest of the site remains editorial and warm.

### OG and social assets

- OG images should tell the positioning story quickly: bold idea, capability framing, and clean contrast.
- Keep backgrounds stable and use a clear visual hierarchy (no text overload).
