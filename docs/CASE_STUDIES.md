## 01. Flexpay
**Promotional Video Production**
*An animated promotional campaign for a financial wellness platform empowering employees with instant access to earned wages.*

Flexpay is a pioneering financial wellness platform that allows employees to access their earned wages in real time. Radical Thinking was brought in to create a comprehensive video production campaign that could distill this innovative concept into a highly engaging, digestible visual asset.

### 01 The challenge
Financial technology can often feel clinical or complex to the end-user. Flexpay needed to clearly communicate a dual-sided value proposition in a single, cohesive informative video series: it had to simultaneously build trust with corporate employers and drive enthusiastic adoption among their employees. 

### 02 The solution
Radical Thinking engineered and produced a dynamic series of animated explainer videos. Skipping over-engineered conceptual phases, we focused on high-impact visual storytelling that mapped out exactly what Flexpay is and how it functions for both sides of the ecosystem. The animations broke down the mechanics of earned wage access into simple, relatable scenarios.

### 03 The outcome
The resulting asset package was deployed across Flexpay’s core digital channels, including YouTube, active social media campaigns, and their primary web platform. The videos significantly enhanced their B2B and B2C marketing efforts, providing the sales and growth teams with a highly effective, frictionless tool to explain the product in seconds.

## 05. 1001 Inventions Games
**Interactive Experiential Exhibition Development**
*A high-tech, multi-device interactive installation connecting physical creativity with digital real-time projection mapping.*

As part of a long-term strategic partnership with 1001 Inventions, Radical Thinking was tasked with creating an immersive educational game tracking the historical journeys and animal recordings of early historical scientists.

### 01 The challenge
The objective was to make historical scientific exploration deeply engaging for young children. The technical challenge lay in creating a seamless, real-time bridge between individual hand-held devices (iPads) and a massive, shared environment projection screen, ensuring zero latency while maintaining an intuitive user flow for kids.

### 02 The solution
Radical Thinking built a custom, fully interactive hardware-software ecosystem. We engineered a dedicated local server module capable of instantly pulling digital artwork from multiple iPads simultaneously. Children painted historical wildlife characters on their tablets and seamlessly "dragged" them into a living, animated digital habitat projected on the main gallery wall.

### 03 The outcome
An exceptionally successful experiential installation where custom-colored animals interacted within their digital habitat for several minutes. The project delivered a deeply memorable educational impact, allowing children to proudly showcase their imaginative creations to parents and teachers while interacting directly with historical narratives.
